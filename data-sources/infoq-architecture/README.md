## Editorial & Curation Policies
1. **News & Case Study**: Includes all non-sponsored news articles, presentations, and the monthly spotlight Case Study.
2. **Exclusions**:
   - Commercial / sponsored vendor content (`/vendorcontent/`).
   - InfoQ certification and training advertisements (`certification.qconferences.com`).
3. **Thematic Metadata**:
   Each issue centers on an editorial theme (e.g. *Building Data and AI Platforms*), which is preserved in `issue_title` and `article.metadata["theme"]`.
